Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Glimbo: Block Puzzle ("Glimbo", "the app") is made by an independent developer in Sweden. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play.

The short version

Glimbo does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game progress and settings (levels, stars, coins, boosters, collected block styles, language and sound settings) are saved only on your own device, so you can continue where you left off. In the iPhone and Android apps a copy is also kept in the app's own storage on the device, so it survives when the system clears temporary files. None of this leaves your device and we cannot see it.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or photos.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not use your device's advertising identifier.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone, because we do not have any.
  • The game works offline; its fonts, music and sounds are part of the app.

Children

Glimbo is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Deleting your data

You can erase all progress at any time in the game under Settings → "Erase my progress", or by deleting the app.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles data, for example optional analytics or advertising, we will update this policy and the privacy details in the App Store and Google Play before that version is released.

Contact
