Support

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Write to glimbogame@gmail.com and tell us your phone model and what happened. We read every message.

How do I play?

Drag pieces onto the 8×8 board. A full row or column clears. Clear again within three moves to grow your combo. Levels have goals such as gems, ice or a boss to beat; open "How to play" on the menu for a short tour.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. It is not linked to an account, so it does not move to a new phone and is lost if you delete the app.

How do I start over?

Settings → "Erase my progress", then tap again to confirm.

Can I turn off the seasonal themes, sounds or motion?

Yes, all of them in Settings: seasonal themes, sound, music, bigger text, color symbols, less motion and a relaxed mode with extra moves.
